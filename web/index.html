How It Works

Four steps from your voice to a spoken answer.

🎤

1 — Speak or Type

Alexa+ or the web demo captures your question via voice or text input.

→
🔌

2 — MCP Server

The question is sent over Streamable HTTP to the self-hosted MCP endpoint at /mcp.

→
🧭

3 — Persona Router

Keyword scoring selects the best expert — VC, CTO, legal, healthcare, and seven more.

→
☁️

4 — Amazon Bedrock

The chosen persona's system prompt + your question are sent to Claude Haiku. The short, voice-ready answer is returned and read aloud.
